Learnings we actually hit

  • Doorman first. Five algos with no allow(key) fails Senior LLD.
  • Instant + Clock. LocalTime.toSecondOfDay() breaks at midnight.
  • Keep remainder. lastRefill = reqTime after integer divide silently kills refill/leak.
  • Snap windows with + n * window, not = reqTime.
  • computeIfAbsent — the race is who owns the instance for a key.
  • No Singleton on a stateless factory. Inject one RateLimiter.
  • Token ≠ Leaky. Tickets vs water. Do not refill and leak the same number.
  • Java: configOf.apply(k), Main::getConfig, this::createFor.

Memorize

Doorman first. Numbers not Tier. Instant not LocalTime. Keep remainder. Decision not boolean. Enum factory, no singleton. computeIfAbsent. Say 2× burst. Redis is a follow-up.
